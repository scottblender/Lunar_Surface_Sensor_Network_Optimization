\subsection{CLPS Deployment Sites and Candidate Locations}
\label{subsec:clps_sites}

NASA's CLPS initiative provides opportunities to deliver optical sensing instruments to the lunar surface through commercial landers. Blender, Hodorowski, and Singh used CLPS destination regions to contextualize terrain-informed observer placement over the lunar southern hemisphere, with a restricted south-polar domain retained for comparison \cite{BlenderHodorowskiSinghAAS}. The present study extends this deployment context by adopting representative CLPS locations as candidate sites for a fixed three-sensor network and as reference locations for geographically constrained sensor relocation. The candidate set includes mission anchors associated with Intuitive Machines IM-3 and Firefly Blue Ghost Missions 1, 2, and 3, together with representative south-polar locations near Mons Mouton and Schr\"odinger Basin. These candidates comprise an as-landed coordinate, published mission targets, and regional study proxies, and therefore represent deployment reference locations with differing levels of positional specificity. Each candidate is defined by its planetocentric latitude, east-positive longitude, and DEM-derived terrain elevation, with observation availability evaluated using the constraints introduced in Section~\ref{sec:preliminaries}. Figure~\ref{fig:clps_study_sites} illustrates the candidate locations, which also define the centers of the 100-km circular placement regions considered in Section~\ref{subsec:design_domains}.
